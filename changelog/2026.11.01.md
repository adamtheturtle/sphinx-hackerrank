# 2026.11.01 (2026-11-01)

## Changes

- Identify guide parts independently of their selected content, with caller-defined minimum counts and required selections that reject missing, ambiguous, or empty content.
  Support alternative heading spellings, conditional group requirements, shared leading sections, part-body selections, and named layouts.
  Consumers migrating from `part_marker` must supply `part_pattern` and wrap heading choices in `SectionSelection`.
  The public sample now exercises automatic composition in installed-wheel and browser checks.

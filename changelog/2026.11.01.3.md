# 2026.11.01.3 (2026-11-01)

## Changes

- Remove regular-expression part selection and minimum-part counts from guideline layouts and RST recipes.
  List every part heading explicitly with `part_headings` in Python, `:parts:` on `interviewer-guideline`, and `:required-for:` on groups.
  Missing or duplicate headings fail the build, and punctuation is literal.
  The `part_pattern` and `minimum_parts` Python fields and the `:part-pattern:`, `:required-for-pattern:`, and `:minimum-parts:` directive options are no longer supported.
